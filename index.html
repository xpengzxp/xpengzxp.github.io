Research

IROS 2026 WorkshopCreativity in Assembly

StackGen3D: Stable 3D Block Stack Generation from Multiview Silhouettes

Xiaopeng Zhang*, Luzhe Sun*, Yiqing Xu, Matthew R. Walter · *equal contribution

Given silhouettes from several views and an inventory of blocks, StackGen3D generates 3D block stacks that stand on their own. Block poses come from conditional flow matching; training data is curated with a physics-informed GNN and verified in MuJoCo. A simulator-free, AABB-based inference step raises stability from 45.3% to 72.4%.
